# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want UI Importer Pro spaces to be welcoming, useful, and safe. Be respectful,
give constructive feedback, and remember that users have different levels of
Roblox, Photoshop, Figma, and programming experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hate speech, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, spam, impersonation, and publishing someone else's private
artwork or information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
is unsafe or disruptive. Serious or repeated violations may result in a
permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct problems privately to the project owner or moderators. Include
links or screenshots where safe. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.

This policy applies in the GitHub repository, the official Discord server, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.
